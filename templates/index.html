About Me

I'm a Site Reliability & DevOps Engineer with 7+ years keeping production systems healthy across AWS, Azure, GCP, and on-premise Linux and Windows servers.

My strongest work happens in the middle of an incident. During a live bot attack I traced the real impact past the bots to a caching misconfiguration and slow Kubernetes autoscaling, and gave the client a clear fix. Today I'm a Senior Cloud Solutions Engineer at Sitecore, where I support a multi-region Azure setup with cross-region database replication.

I build the monitoring that catches problems early (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, Datadog, Azure Monitor) and automate repetitive operations with Bash, Python, and PowerShell. Production changes I lead go out from runbooks proven in test, with clear rollback points. I also write about DevOps and cloud on Medium.
